DishCourse Community

House Rules 🍽️

Good food brings people together. Good conversation should too. Pull up a chair, share your thoughts, and help us keep DishCourse welcoming for everyone at the table.

🥄 Critique the Dish, Not the Cook

Disagree with a recipe, technique, or opinion all you want — just keep it respectful. There's always room for a different taste at the table.

🌶️ Keep It Flavorful, Not Foul

A little spice makes a conversation interesting. Personal attacks, insults, and disrespect do not.

🍰 Give Credit Where It's Due

Sharing someone else's recipe, technique, or idea? Give them their flowers — and their credit.

🥘 Don't Spam the Kitchen

Nobody ordered 37 servings of the same post. Keep discussions relevant and avoid repetitive or promotional spam.

🍴 Everyone Gets a Seat

New cooks, seasoned chefs, foodies, recipe experimenters, and people who just really love to eat are all welcome here.

❤️ Enjoy the DishCourse

Ask questions. Share what you know. Learn something new. Laugh a little. And most importantly, enjoy the conversation.
